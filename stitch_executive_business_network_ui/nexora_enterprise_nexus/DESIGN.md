## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the precision and operational momentum of modern enterprise dealmaking. Built for an ecosystem where institutional capital, corporate enterprise, and high-growth ventures converge, the visual signature blends high-density operational utility with prestige finance credibility.

The aesthetic philosophy bridges **Minimalist Precision** with **Engineered Tactility**:
- Crisp, hairline architectural scaffolding establishes rigor, order, and non-negotiable trust.
- Controlled micro-surfaces and selective glass transitions evoke modern enterprise efficiency without sacrificing readability.
- Purposeful kinetic cues convey real-time algorithmic matching, asset liquidity, and verified institutional pedigree.

The UI avoids decorative clutter in favor of high-yield data density, crystalline typographic hierarchies, and deliberate bursts of growth-oriented color accents. The resulting interface feels authoritative, dynamic, and effortlessly performant.

## Colors

The color system enforces strict semantic segregation between institutional authority, verified liquidity, and urgent opportunity triggers.

### Architectural Canvas & Foundations
- **Base Deep Navy (`#091E42`)**: Anchors primary navigation, structural headers, high-contrast badges, and primary buttons. Denotes executive governance and enterprise stability.
- **Midnight Slate (`#0F172A`)**: High-contrast typography layer ensuring total clarity across analytical feeds and ledger tables.
- **Pure White Canvas (`#FFFFFF`) & Crisp Slate Floor (`#F8FAFC`)**: Baseline canvas layers providing pristine contrast, segmented by hairline dividers (`#E2E8F0` and `#F1F5F9`).

### Signal Accents
- **Growth Emerald (`#10B981`) & Deep Teal (`#059669`)**: Used exclusively for deal matching, algorithmic affinity scores, capital verification badges, and positive momentum indicators. Never used decoratively.
- **Priority Amber / Coral (`#F59E0B` / `#F97316`)**: Reserved for deal room notifications, term-sheet expiries, exclusive syndicated allocations, and urgent attention states.
- **Muted Slate Fill (`#F1F5F9`)**: Subdued container surface for nested data grids, auxiliary metric panels, and inactive chip selectors.

## Typography

Typographic hierarchy enforces rapid information scanning and transactional clarity. 

- **Geist** serves as the core linguistic engine across all headlines, body copy, and UI controls. Its balanced geometric construction and low horizontal expansion allow dense layouts to remain effortless and uncluttered.
- **JetBrains Mono** is introduced for institutional data arrays, financial metrics, delta percentages, timestamps, and tracking badges. This sharp distinction immediately signals analytical verification and machine-driven matching logic.
- Numerical data points in dashboards, cap tables, and matching indexes should systematically employ tabular figures (`tnum`) to maintain vertical alignment across complex data grids.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is engineered using an adaptable 12-column dynamic framework designed to comfortably toggle between content ingestion feeds and high-density financial data terminals.

- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12-column layout with 24px gutters, locked inside a 1440px maximum content shell. Primary sidebars use fixed 280px rails, reserving fluid space for deal cards and matching matrices.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout with 16px gutters and 24px screen-edge margins. Navigation drawers collapse into an interactive floating command rail.
- **Mobile (<= 767px)**: 4-column layout with 12px gutters and 16px screen-edge margins. Complex tables convert to stacked, swipeable insight cards with absolute-positioned priority tags.

Vertical pacing follows a strict 4px/8px modular cadence. Component interiors adhere to structured paddings:
- Global standard cards: 24px uniform padding.
- High-density feeds and table rows: 12px vertical padding with 16px horizontal offsets.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layers** combined with **Crisp Hairline Scaffolding** and ambient slate diffusion. Depth is a functional signal of interactive availability and z-axis priority, not decorative styling.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: `#F8FAFC`. Base environment background.
- **Level 1 (Structural Surface)**: `#FFFFFF` surfaced with a 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`). Used for standard cards, lists, and form surfaces. No blur or shadow; depth is expressed purely through surface separation and sharp borders.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Hover)**: `#FFFFFF` with a 1px solid border (`#CBD5E1`), elevated by an ambient shadow: `0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(9, 30, 66, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(9, 30, 66, 0.03)`. Used for hovered cards and inline expansion panels.
- **Level 3 (Command & Popovers)**: `#FFFFFF` with border (`#CBD5E1`), elevated by: `0px 12px 28px -4px rgba(9, 30, 66, 0.08), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(9, 30, 66, 0.03)`. Used for filter flyouts, combobox panels, and match details.
- **Level 4 (Modal Deal Rooms & Overlays)**: Surface backed by `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` with background `rgba(9, 30, 66, 0.40)`. Modal windows carry `0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(9, 30, 66, 0.18)` and an internal inset top highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`).

## Shapes

The geometric vernacular uses an engineered structural contour (`roundedness: 2`). This provides a modern, humanized finish while preserving strict corporate architecture.

- **Main Deal & Match Cards**: `rounded-xl` (16px / 1.0rem) ensures high surface readability and frames interactive modules cleanly.
- **Action Buttons, Form Inputs, & Interactive Dropdowns**: `rounded-lg` (8px / 0.5rem) aligns controls to crisp touch targets.
- **Status Indicators, Match Score Badges, & Categorical Tags**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`rounded-full`) create an immediate visual contrast against geometric card corners.
- **Table Surface Clamps**: External container bounds lock to `rounded-xl` with internal cells remaining unrounded for boundary precision.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Midnight Navy (`#091E42`) solid fill, white text, subtle 1px inset highlight. Hover: `#0F172A`. Active: transform scale(0.99).
- **Growth Trigger (Opportunity Action)**: Emerald (`#10B981`) fill, pure white text, hover: `#059669`. Used for direct connect, deal introduction, and term-sheet validation.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Clean `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), `#091E42` text. Hover: border color shifts to `#CBD5E1`, background to `#F8FAFC`.
- **Ghost**: Transparent fill, `#475569` text. Hover: `#F1F5F9` background, `#091E42` text.

### Cards & Opportunity Containers
- Built on `rounded-xl` white containers encased in a 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
- Header zones partition institutional identifiers (logo, ticker, industry taxonomy) alongside verified status badges.
- Bottom action shelves feature low-saturation backgrounds (`#F8FAFC`) with hair-line borders separating metadata from primary matching triggers.

### Badges & Opportunity Chips
- **Match Score Pill**: JetBrains Mono numerical indicator. Pill styling uses low-opacity Emerald base (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`) with high-contrast text (`#047857`) and a 1px border (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`).
- **High-Priority Trigger**: Warm Coral pill (`rgba(249, 115, 22, 0.1)`), coral text (`#C2410C`), accompanied by a 6px pulsating dot.
- **Verification Seal**: Navy pill (`#091E42`) with crisp white label and micro checkmark icon.

### Form Inputs & Combobox Selectors
- Clean white inputs framed by 1px `#CBD5E1` borders, 40px height, 8px corner radius.
- Typography: 14px Geist text with `#94A3B8` placeholder.
- Focus State: Border transitions to `#091E42` accompanied by a crisp `0 0 0 3px rgba(9, 30, 66, 0.08)` focus ring. Never native browser outlines.

### Data Tables & Deal Lists
- Alternating zero-tint rows (`#FFFFFF` to `#F8FAFC`) bordered by hairline dividers (`#F1F5F9`).
- Column headers formatted in `label-caps` typography (`JetBrains Mono`, 11px, tracking +0.06em, `#64748B`).
- Numerical cells feature right-aligned tabular figures with color-coded directional metrics (+18.4% Emerald / -3.2% Rose).
