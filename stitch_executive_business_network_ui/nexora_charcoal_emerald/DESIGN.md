## Brand & Style

This design system targets modern B2B enterprise applications that require precision, high analytical density, and effortless trust. The visual tone is authoritative yet vibrant: deep structural neutrals anchor complex workflows, while concentrated hits of emerald drive decisive action.

### Design Movement
The core aesthetic fuses **Corporate / Modern** engineering precision with a crisp, low-noise editorial finish. It eliminates superfluous embellishments in favor of immaculate typography, high contrast ratios, architectural alignment, and deliberate micro-interactions.

### Key Tenets
- **Architectural Grounding:** Heavy structural containers (sidebars, high-level headers) utilize deep charcoal to ground navigation and framing.
- **Actionable Vitality:** Emerald green is reserved strictly for affirmative actions, forward momentum, status success, and active navigation indicators.
- **Legibility First:** High information density without visual clutter, leveraging generous whitespace against structured hairline dividers.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for prolonged operational focus, offering clear contrast separation between structural framing, canvas depth, and interactive focal points.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1E2229`):** Deep Charcoal. Reserved for primary structural shells, global navigation sidebars, persistent app headers, and key authoritative UI regions.
- **Tertiary / Accent (`#4A9D4E`):** Emerald Green. The energetic linchpin of the interface. Applied to primary call-to-actions, active toggles, completed indicators, and high-priority metrics.
- **Secondary (`#64748B`):** Sage Slate. Serves non-interactive iconography, tertiary controls, sub-navigation tabs, and structural context indicators.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`):** Ink Black. High-legibility base for primary body text, table row headers, and prominent metric values.

### Surface & Border Scale
- **Canvas / Page Background:** Soft Mint White (`#F4F7F6`) provides subtle organic warmth compared to clinical cold grays.
- **Surface Layer (Cards, Popovers, Modals):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Hairline Borders:** Light Slate (`#E2E8F0`) for standard boundaries; Mid Slate (`#CBD5E1`) for interactive inputs and nested structures.
- **Muted Text / Secondary Copy:** Slate Gray (`#475569`).

## Typography

Geist powers the entire typographical hierarchy, offering geometric rigor, uniform tabular figures, and optimized vertical metrics suited for complex enterprise dashboards.

### Type Guidance
- **Hierarchy & Tracking:** Tight negative letter spacing is applied to display and headline styles (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) to maintain optical density. Labels and micro-metadata utilize positive tracking (`+0.04em`) for instant legibility in data grids.
- **Numbers and Metrics:** Numeric indicators in analytical widgets, tabular data, and KPI scorecards must utilize tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal layout shift.
- **Hierarchy Pairings:** Primary headlines should always be rendered in Deep Charcoal or Ink Black; subheads and structural field labels transition to Sage Slate.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8-point base grid (with a 4-point micro step for compact controls and table cells).

### Grid Model
- **Desktop (1280px+):** Fluid 12-column grid with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) screen margins. Collapsible fixed-width global sidebar (240px expanded, 64px collapsed).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Fluid 8-column grid with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1.5rem` (24px) margins. Navigation transforms into an off-canvas drawer or consolidated top bar.
- **Mobile (<768px):** Fluid 4-column single-stack layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters and margins. Key actions anchor to sticky bottom action bars.

### Density Tiers
- **Comfortable:** Default for configuration pages, settings, and high-level summaries (`space-base` to `space-xl` internal component padding).
- **Compact:** Standard for operational data grids, logs, and filter arrays (`space-xs` to `space-sm` row padding).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **low-contrast outlines** paired with surgical, ultra-diffused ambient drop shadows. This prevents UI muddiness and keeps data front and center.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the Mint White page background (`#F4F7F6`).
- **Level 1 (Surface / Cards):** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px solid hairline border in `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating / Dropdowns / Popovers):** `#FFFFFF` surface with `#CBD5E1` border. Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals / Drawers):** `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a subtle `#1E2229` backdrop overlay at 40% opacity. Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a strict `0.5rem` (8px / `ROUND_EIGHT`) corner geometry across base interactive controls, balancing clinical enterprise structure with modern softness.

### Corner Application
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges, Tabs):** `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Surface Cards & Modular Panels:** `0.5rem` (8px) default; complex dashboards may step up to `0.75rem` (12px) for primary outer frames.
- **Pills / Status Dots:** Fully rounded (`9999px`) reserved only for micro-tags, notification counters, and circular avatar indicators.
